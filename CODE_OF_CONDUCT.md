# Code of Conduct

Be decent. Treat people with respect, assume good intent, and keep discussion on
the work.

Harassment, personal attacks, and other unwelcome behavior aren't tolerated — in
issues, pull requests, or anywhere else around the project.

If someone's out of line, email desmj@pm.me. Reports stay private. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions that don't fit, and ban repeat offenders.
